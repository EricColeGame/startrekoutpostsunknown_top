That last row trips people up constantly. Family Sharing lets another account borrow your library when you're not playing — it does not let two captains run a colony together at the same time. It's a library feature, not a game mode.

## Why the Star Trek Outposts Unknown Multiplayer Question Won't Go Away

Three things keep this rumor alive, according to community reports and player experience in launch-week threads.

**Genre expectations.** Outpost builders and colony sims live on the same shelf as games that *do* offer co-op, and Steam's own "More like this" rail on the store page points toward titles in that same management space. Players reasonably assume the category standard applies.

**The bundle.** *Outposts Unknown* is sold in a discounted bundle alongside *Star Trek: Voyager - Across the Unknown*, which makes it look like part of a connected, possibly shared, Star Trek gaming suite. It isn't — they're separate games with separate saves.

**The fantasy itself.** The pitch — "lead your crew across strange new worlds" — sounds like something you'd do with friends. Assigning tasks, developing officers, splitting research priorities: those are naturally collaborative verbs. The design language invites the question even though the feature isn't there.

It's also worth noting the game launched to Mixed reviews on Steam, and a portion of that early feedback involves players discovering the scope of the package after purchase. Checking the mode list first is the single best protection against that.

## How to Share the Colony Experience Without a Multiplayer Mode

No co-op doesn't mean no shared experience. Communities around single-player builders have developed reliable workarounds, and they work well here because the game is real-time with pause — meaning you can stop and talk through decisions without losing ground.

| Workaround | How it works | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord screen share | One player drives, everyone else calls research and build orders | Groups of 2–4 who want a "writer's room" feel |
| Parallel colonies | Both players start fresh, compare bases and crew outcomes after each session | Friendly competition and theory-crafting |
| Hot-seat sessions | Hand off the mouse at natural story beats | Couples and roommates sharing one PC |
| Challenge runs | Same seed, same difficulty, race to stabilize the outpost | Content creators and community events |
| Shared notes doc | Track tech tree priorities and officer assignments together | Long-running playthroughs with a partner |

The pause-and-plan structure is the secret sauce. Because you can freeze time to reassign crew and queue structures, a second person can genuinely contribute decisions rather than just watch. Set a rule — for example, one player owns research and the other owns crew morale — and the session starts to feel like a two-person command team.

## What a Real Co-Op Mode Would Need to Work

This is squarely in wishlist territory — nothing here is announced, and the developers have not committed to post-launch multiplayer. But thinking through the design problem explains why a co-op patch isn't as trivial as flipping a switch.

| Design challenge | Why it's hard in this game |
| --- | --- |
| Shared time controls | The game leans on real-time-with-pause; two players pausing independently breaks flow |
| Officer ownership | Crew development is personal and story-driven — who owns which arc? |
| Save authority | One host must own progression or saves desync quickly |
| Difficulty scaling | The cosmic threat is tuned for one colony's output curve |
| Narrative focus | The campaign is built around a single captain's choices |

Community reports suggest most of the demand is for a simple two-player shared outpost rather than anything competitive. That's a smaller ask than full multiplayer infrastructure — but it's still a design decision the studio would have to make deliberately, and there's no indication it's on the table right now.

## FAQ

**Is Star Trek Outposts Unknown multiplayer?**

No. At launch, Star Trek Outposts Unknown multiplayer support does not exist — the game is single-player only, with no online co-op, local co-op, or competitive modes listed on its store page.

**Can two people play Star Trek Outposts Unknown together using Steam Family Sharing?**

Not simultaneously. Family Sharing lets another account access your library when you're not using it, but it does not create a shared session. Two players cannot run the same colony at the same time.

**Will a co-op mode be added in a future update?**

Nothing has been announced. The developers have not committed publicly to post-launch multiplayer, so treat any claims about an upcoming co-op patch as unverified until you see it on an official channel.

**What's the closest thing to Star Trek Outposts Unknown multiplayer right now?**

Screen sharing over Discord while one player drives, or running parallel colonies and comparing results. Both preserve the decision-making that makes the game interesting without requiring any official multiplayer support.
