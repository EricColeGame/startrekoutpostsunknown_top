## Gameplay and Story: Building a Future in the X'Lehari System

The setup is straightforward in the best way. You are placed in charge of an outpost in the X'Lehari System, tasked with rendering aid to a fragile civilization facing an impending catastrophe, all while a mysterious cosmic force threatens life in the region. The story is original and set in the 23rd century, in the same era depicted by *Star Trek: Strange New Worlds*.

Mechanically, the game sits at the intersection of several familiar genres. The store tags tell the story better than a marketing paragraph can:

| Tag | What It Implies for Play |
| --- | --- |
| Colony Sim | You manage population needs, not just structures |
| Base Building | Layout and adjacency decisions shape efficiency |
| Resource Management | Supply chains gate your expansion pace |
| Real-Time with Pause | You can plan without twitch reflexes deciding fights |
| Choices Matter | Narrative branches and crew morale carry consequences |
| Single-player | No co-op or competitive mode to worry about |

A few practical notes for your first session. Build your research capacity early, because tech unlocks appear to gate the more interesting structures. Pay attention to crew morale during dangerous encounters — the game explicitly tests officer prowess and morale, so treating your crew as disposable stat blocks is likely to backfire. And use the pause function liberally; real-time-with-pause systems reward players who plan in bursts rather than react constantly.

There are 53 achievements to chase, which suggests a reasonably meaty campaign with room for repeat objectives. Steam Cloud support and Family Sharing are both enabled, so progress carries across machines and household libraries.
