## What You're Actually Playing Once It's Installed

Setting aside the platform question, the game itself is a narrative-driven outpost builder — the store bills it as the first outpost builder set in the Star Trek universe. You construct research facilities across strange new worlds, work through tech and building trees, and manage a crew whose morale and competence are tested by dangerous encounters.

The story is original and set in the 23rd century, in the era depicted by *Star Trek: Strange New Worlds*. You explore the X'Lehari System, assign tasks to officers, and develop them over time while a mysterious cosmic force threatens a new ally and, eventually, all life in the system. Iconic technology from the franchise shows up as tools rather than set dressing.

Key gameplay pillars:

- **Base building with consequences** — structure choices shape which research paths open up.
- **Crew management** — officers need development, and morale is a live variable.
- **Real-time with pause** — plan, then let the simulation run.
- **Narrative framing** — an original story rather than a sandbox-only experience.
- **Exploration loop** — the X'Lehari System is the map, not a menu.
