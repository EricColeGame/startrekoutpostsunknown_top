Two things jump out immediately. First, **16 GB of RAM is required at both tiers** — there's no 8 GB floor here. Second, the install footprint is tiny at 10 GB, which is unusually lean for a base-building game with a full narrative campaign.

For context on what you're installing: the game ships with 53 Steam achievements, Steam Cloud support, Family Sharing, and eight supported languages (English plus French, German, Spanish — Spain, Polish, Portuguese — Brazil, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish — Latin America), with English as the only full-audio option.

## Minimum vs. Recommended: What Actually Changes

The gap between the two tiers is almost entirely CPU and GPU. Memory and storage are identical, so you don't need to buy anything extra in those departments if you already clear the minimum.

### Processor comparison

| Tier | AMD Option | Intel Option | What It Signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | Ryzen 5 2600 | Core i5-8500 | Six cores from the 2018 era; fine for a modest colony |
| Recommended | Ryzen 7 7800X3D | Core i5-14600K | Modern high-clock chips for large, late-game bases |

The recommended CPUs are notably newer than the recommended GPUs — a Ryzen 7 7800X3D is a current-generation gaming flagship. That's a strong hint that the simulation layer (colonist pathing, resource ticks, event logic) scales with CPU headroom as your outpost grows, which is typical for colony sims.

### Graphics card comparison

| Tier | NVIDIA | AMD | Roughly Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | GTX 1660 (6 GB) | RX 5600 XT (8 GB) | 1080p with settings tuned down |
| Recommended | RTX 3060 | RX 5700 XT | 1080p high, or higher resolutions with adjustments |

Note that the minimum AMD card carries 8 GB of VRAM while the NVIDIA card has 6 GB. If you're on a 4 GB card from an older generation, treat that as below spec rather than "probably fine."

### A quick reality check on the OS line

The minimum lists Windows 10 Version 21H1 specifically. The recommended row only says "64-bit processor and operating system," which is vague — it doesn't name Windows 11. If you're on Windows 11, you're almost certainly fine, but if you're still on an early Windows 10 build, run `winver` and update before launch day. This is the single most common reason a machine that "should" run the game gets a compatibility warning.

## How to Check Your PC Before You Buy

You don't need to install anything to verify the **Star Trek Outposts Unknown system requirements** on your own hardware. Work through this checklist in order.

| Step | What to Do | What You're Confirming |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Press Windows key, type `dxdiag`, hit Enter | OS version, CPU model, and total RAM on the System tab |
| 2 | Open the Display or Render tab in dxdiag | Exact GPU name and dedicated VRAM |
| 3 | Open Settings → System → Storage | Free space on your target drive (need 10 GB+) |
| 4 | Open Task Manager → Performance → Memory | Whether you actually have 16 GB installed, not 8 GB |
| 5 | Compare your GPU name to the table above | Whether you're above, at, or below the minimum card |
| 6 | Update your GPU driver | Avoids launch-day crashes unrelated to raw specs |

A few practical notes on reading your own results:

- **Laptop GPUs are not desktop GPUs.** A "RTX 3060 Laptop GPU" is a different, slower chip than a desktop RTX 3060. Treat mobile parts as one tier lower than the name suggests.
- **Integrated graphics won't cut it.** The minimum is a discrete card with 6–8 GB of VRAM, so Intel Iris Xe or AMD Radeon integrated graphics fall well short.
- **"16 GB RAM" means installed, not available.** If you have 16 GB but 4 GB is permanently eaten by background apps, close them before playing.

If your CPU and GPU both clear the minimum and you have 16 GB of RAM plus 10 GB free, you should be able to play. You just may need to lower settings once your colony gets big.

## FAQ: Star Trek Outposts Unknown System Requirements

**Can I run Star Trek Outposts Unknown system requirements on 8 GB of RAM?**
No. The **Star Trek Outposts Unknown system requirements** list 16 GB at both minimum and recommended tiers, with no lower-spec option. Adding a matching memory kit is the single most effective upgrade if you're currently at 8 GB.

**Is a GTX 1660 really enough?**
Yes, according to the official minimum. The GTX 1660 (6 GB) or RX 5600 XT (8 GB) is the stated floor, which should translate to 1080p play with settings reduced. Expect to lower detail once your outpost grows and more objects are on screen.

**Do I need Windows 11?**
No. The minimum specifies Windows 10 Version 21H1, and the recommended row only asks for a 64-bit OS without naming a version. Windows 10 up to date or Windows 11 both satisfy the listed requirements.

**Does the game work on Steam Deck?**
Compatibility is currently listed as Unknown on Steam, meaning it hasn't been verified. Given the 16 GB RAM requirement and the CPU-driven simulation, it's safer to wait for an official rating before buying for handheld play.

**How much disk space should I actually free up?**
The listed requirement is 10 GB, which is small. Aim for 15–20 GB free so saves, patches, and shader caches have room to grow without you managing space mid-campaign.
