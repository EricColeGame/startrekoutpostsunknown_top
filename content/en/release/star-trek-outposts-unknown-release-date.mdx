## What Kind of Game Is It, Exactly?

Marketing language can be vague, so it helps to be concrete: this is a narrative-driven outpost builder. You are not commanding a starship in combat — you are constructing and managing research facilities on strange new worlds while a story unfolds around your crew.

The core loop breaks down into three interlocking pieces:

1. **Research and build.** You work through tech and building trees, choosing which structures to prioritize. The Steam tags lean heavily on colony sim, resource management, city builder, and base building, so expect supply chains and layout decisions to matter.
2. **Explore the X'Lehari System.** The game is set in an original region of space, and exploration is a first-class system rather than set dressing.
3. **Manage people.** Crew morale and "prowess" are explicit mechanics, and officers can be developed over time. Dangerous encounters test both, and survival is not guaranteed by good infrastructure alone.

The narrative is set in the 23rd century, in the same era depicted in *Star Trek: Strange New Worlds*, and follows a completely original story. Your mission involves rendering aid to a fragile civilization facing an impending catastrophe while dealing with a mysterious cosmic force threatening life in the system. Combat is not the focus; the pressure comes from resource scarcity, crew stress, and story choices. The "Real-Time with Pause" tag confirms you can stop the clock to plan — a welcome inclusion for a management game with this many overlapping systems.

- **Check achievements as a soft tutorial.** With 53 Steam achievements, the list doubles as a rough roadmap of systems the designers consider core.
